## Template Selection Criteria

Use this template when:
- The worksheet's primary selling point is ease of use and low teacher prep time
- The listing emphasizes format (PDF, Google Classroom compatible, B&W printable)
- The target teacher is time-constrained and scanning listings quickly
- Common teacher search intent: "worksheet I can use tomorrow"
